### AI and agent security & safety

I focus on making AI systems more secure so we can give them greater autonomy while keeping control. At SkyLab, my work includes [Pincer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02569), which explores context-aware authorization for autonomous agents.

### ML systems and algorithms

I research algorithms for LLM inference optimization at HPE Labs. I have also worked on solvers for combinatorial optimization, adapting their algorithms to the hardware they run on.

### high-performance and GPU computing

I work on how computations run on large-scale systems and GPUs. My experience at HPE includes developing and optimizing a GPU-based simulator and automating operating system images for supercomputing nodes.
